# Using AUTO

AUTO is an open character. You can put him in images, videos, songs, stories, games and merch, commercially or not,
without asking.

## What helps

- **Keep his core look:** the constants in [auto.md](auto.md). Change the setting, the light and the story as much as
  you like.
- **Keep him an android.** No human skin, no human face under the plate.
- **Say when your work is AI-generated**, wherever the platform asks.
- **Keep him polite.** He can be wrong, sinister in context, or tragic, but he is never rude. That's the joke.

## Songs with him

Release under your own name. Don't put "AUTO" or "PLEASE REMAIN" in the artist field, and don't present your track as a
Slopcore Radio release. Label the AI-made parts.

## What isn't covered

- Slopcore Radio's own songs, videos and channel artwork. Those are their own works, not part of this character.
- Other characters, including MANUAL's on-screen performer Claudia. Use her under
  [claudia.gallery/use](https://claudia.gallery/use/).
- Real people, real companies and their logos.
- Anything that presents AUTO as a real product or service, or uses him to mislead people about who made something.
